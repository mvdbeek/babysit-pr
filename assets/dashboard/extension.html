Install or update

Download the extension

  1. Unzip it where it can stay, such as your home folder. It unpacks to a babysitter-extension folder; an update replaces that folder's files.
  2. The first time: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the folder. After an update: click Reload on its card.

Its ID is always , whichever folder it is loaded from, so pairing survives updates. The extension says when this dashboard has a newer version.

Pair

Pair your extension to start tasks and send follow-ups from your browser.

Copy its ID from the extension settings. Pairing again replaces its previous token.

Keep this token private. It allows task launches and messages to your agents.
